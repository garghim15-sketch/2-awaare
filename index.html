A Note For You

To My Favorite Person

Five months ago, on June 23rd, we met for the very first time waiting at the Akshardham metro station. Who knew we’d end up sitting next to each other on that bus to Kedarnath, starting an endless conversation that still hasn't stopped? From that first hello to standing beside you on November 22nd as our families bless our Roka—every single mile with you has felt like coming home.

Thank you for matching my weirdness, and for turning ordinary road trips and quiet evenings into memories I will hold onto forever.

As we officially begin our #2awaare chapter, I am so endlessly proud of who you are. I promise to always stand right beside you while you take the limelight, because I know you are meant to rule—and I will never let you forget how deeply you are loved.

Forever yours, K & H ♥
