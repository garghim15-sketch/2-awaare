Chapter 07

A Note For You

If someone had told me in Kedarnath that a chance meeting in the mountains would lead us all the way to our Roka, I would have smiled and hoped it was true—and somehow, life made it even better than I imagined.

From trekking down under the moon wishing for two chairs and two cups of chai, to travelling all the way to Gwalior just to sit across from you, to Dalhousie, Khajjiar, Chakrata, and our quiet Punchgaon evenings—every single mile with you has felt effortless. I love our unfiltered madness, our handmade macramé, the cardboard ring I’ll treasure forever, and even the rare, peaceful minutes when the demon is finally asleep.

Today, as we officially begin forever with our families’ blessings, I promise to always be your steady ground and your partner in every unplanned adventure. Thank you for choosing me, for bringing so much warmth into my life, and for walking into this next chapter hand in hand as #2awaare.

— Always yours, H
